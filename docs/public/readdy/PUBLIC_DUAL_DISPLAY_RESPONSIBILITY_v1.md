# Public Dual Display Responsibility v1

## Large PAD / website-aligned experience

- Store/fleet and machine-management presentation.
- Catalog and verified asset presentation.
- Customer branding, reports and topology according to role.
- Authenticated maintenance/settings entry points as approved UI shells.
- Rich multimedia and read-only recommendation experience.

## Small local display

- Focused customer interaction and immediate local status.
- Minimal accepted local operation and maintenance presentation.

It does not own cloud administration, full catalog publishing, financial reports, identity allocation or unrestricted engineering tools.

## Shared rules

- Both displays use the same opaque machine/configuration/transaction references supplied by approved services.
- A stale display refreshes or rejects; it never overwrites newer state.
- Status conflict becomes `UNKNOWN` or `RECONCILING`.
- Customer flows hide internal identifiers.
- The large display contains no motor/register logic; the small display contains no cloud/business administration.

Exact MCU/Gateway arbitration, registers, commands and reverse-engineering evidence are private and intentionally absent.
